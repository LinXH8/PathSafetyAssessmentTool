# 1.2 User Login & Account Management

## Creating a New Profile

1. On the Landing Page, click the **+** button in the Profiles panel.
2. Enter a **Username**, your **LTA Employee Email** (e.g. `user@lta.gov.sg`), your **Division**, and a **4–12 digit numeric PIN** (numbers only).
3. Click **Create Profile**. The profile is saved and you are automatically logged in.

> **Tip:** Your email is what you log in with, and it is also used to reset a forgotten PIN. It is not shown to other users.

---

## Signing In

The Landing Page does not list personal profiles. You log in by typing your details.

1. Click **Log in with email and PIN**.
2. Enter the email on your profile and your PIN.
3. Click **Log In**. You are taken to your **Projects** page, with all previously saved projects available.

> If your profile was created before emails were introduced, type your username in the email box instead.

---

## The Shared Islandwide Profile

The **Islandwide Data** profile is the one profile shown on the Landing Page. It holds the autocoded islandwide project and is open to everyone.

1. Click the **Islandwide Data** card, then **Log in as Islandwide Data**.
2. Enter the shared PIN provided by your administrator.

This profile cannot be edited or deleted, and the project inside it cannot be deleted or renamed. To work on your own copy, share the project from Islandwide to your own profile.

---

## Managing Your Account

After logging in, click **My Account** in the left-hand sidebar (above **Logout**) to:

- change your username, email, or division
- change your PIN
- delete your profile

Each action asks for your current PIN. Deleting a profile removes all projects in it.

---

## Switching Between Accounts

- Click **Logout** in the left-hand sidebar at any time. You are returned to the Landing Page.
- Click **Log in with email and PIN** and enter the other profile's email and PIN.

---

## Session Behaviour

- You stay logged in on that browser until you click **Logout**, even if you close the tab. On a shared computer, log out when you are done.
- All tabs in the same browser share the same login; logging out in one tab logs you out in the others.
- Your projects, images, and results are kept — logging out does not delete any data.

---

## Forgotten PIN

1. On the Landing Page, click **Log in with email and PIN**, then **Forgot PIN?**.
2. Enter your **username**, the **email** on your profile, and a **new PIN**.
3. Click **Reset PIN**, then log in with the new PIN.

No email is sent; the email is only checked against the one on your profile. If you do not remember your username or email, ask your administrator.
